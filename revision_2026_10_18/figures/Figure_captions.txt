Figure S1. Area affected by extreme-month classes across Pakistan (1985–2025).
(A) National monthly area affected (km²) by hot–dry (HD), hot–wet (HW), hot-only, and dry-only months, computed by summing grid-cell surface area flagged each month.
(B) Elevation-zone monthly area affected (km²) for HD and HW months across five elevation regimes (<500, 500–1500, 1500–2500, 2500–3500, ≥3500 m a.s.l.). Areas are calculated on the analysis grid using cell-wise surface area to account for geographic distortion.

Figure S2. Event-aligned NDVI′ trajectories under hot–dry events by elevation regime.
NDVI anomalies (calendar-month z-scores) are aligned to HD event termination (lag 0) and summarised for each elevation band over a fixed pre-event (−6 months) and post-event (+12 months) window. Solid lines indicate the mean trajectory across events; shaded ribbons indicate the interquartile range (IQR). The horizontal reference line denotes baseline conditions (z = 0).

Figure S3. Historical model contribution patterns for the leading SHAP predictors in the environment-only Random Forest model of hot–dry (HD) resistance proxy. Panels show SHAP dependence relationships between each predictor and its SHAP contribution to predicted HD resistance proxy (NDVI anomaly z-score), based on the SHAP prediction contributions sample. Points represent individual HD events (or event-sampled records), and the smoothed curve summarises the average fitted-model contribution across the predictor range. Positive SHAP values indicate an increase in predicted resistance proxy (less NDVI suppression), whereas negative values indicate a lower fitted resistance proxy value. Predictors shown are elevation (elev_m), antecedent vegetation state (ndvi_pre), maximum temperature during HD months (tmax_mean), and climatic water balance during HD months (wb_mean).

Figure S4. Sensitivity of pixel-wise hot–dry (HD) exposure and resistance proxy to alternative event definitions. (A) Fraction of valid pixels experiencing ≥1 HD event under each scenario. (B) Median pixel-wise resistance proxy (NDVI′ z-score; mean within-event minimum per pixel) across scenarios. Scenarios vary the hot/dry percentile thresholds and the dryness proxy (precipitation, climatic water balance, or combined VPD–soil limitation).
